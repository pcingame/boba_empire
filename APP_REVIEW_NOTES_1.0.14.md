# App Review — Notes for Reviewer, bản 1.0.14 (29)

Dán vào **App Store Connect → phiên bản 1.0.14 → App Review Information → Notes** (tiếng Anh).
Giữ nguyên phần GENERAL / EMAIL LINK của `APP_REVIEW_NOTES_1.0.10.md`, đoạn 1.0.11 (ảnh đại diện + báo cáo) và 1.0.12, thêm đoạn dưới. Không có IAP mới, không có SQL mới. Đoạn 1.0.13 (ảnh trong chat Hội) chưa từng có ghi chú riêng nên gộp vào đây.

---

```
WHAT'S NEW IN 1.0.14 (in addition to the notes of 1.0.11 and 1.0.12)

1) Monthly Challenge: 3 goals per calendar month (taps, purchases, accessories
   owned) shown in Daily/Weekly quests. Rewards are virtual gems and one
   in-game accessory. No real-money purchase, gems cannot be cashed out.

2) Hall of Fame: 11 milestone badges on "My profile", derived from the player's
   own progress. Visible only to the player.

3) Welcome-back gift: a player who returns after 3+ days away receives 50-200
   virtual gems (at most once per 7 days) in the offline-earnings popup. If the
   player has allowed notifications, the existing 3-day / 7-day reminder text
   mentions the gift. Notifications are local only (no push server) and require
   the standard system permission prompt; they can be disabled in iOS Settings.

4) Weekly recap: a one-time popup summarising last week's in-game stats.

5) Branches: a city branch is now opened with gems (previously coins); levels
   are still upgraded with coins. Gems can also be earned by playing, so no
   purchase is required to progress. Branch income per level was increased and
   the payback time is shown on each branch card.

6) USER-GENERATED CONTENT - photos in Guild chat (added in 1.0.13)
   Members of a guild (email-linked accounts only) can attach one small JPEG
   (size-limited to 350 KB) to a guild chat message. Photos are shown only
   inside the guild chat (files are stored with random, unguessable names). Moderation: any member can long-press a message
   and choose "Report"; reported messages are hidden together with their photo
   until reviewed, abusive content is removed and the account can be banned.
   Images are never required.

7) Also from 1.0.13: Staff, Mascots and weekend Auctions (virtual items, bought
   and sold with in-game currency only), Regular customers, Weather, Legacy and
   Shop decor. No real-money trading of items between players.
```
