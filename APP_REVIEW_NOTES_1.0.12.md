# App Review — Notes for Reviewer, bản 1.0.12 (26)

Dán vào **App Store Connect → phiên bản 1.0.12 → App Review Information → Notes** (tiếng Anh).
Giữ nguyên phần GENERAL / EMAIL LINK của `APP_REVIEW_NOTES_1.0.10.md` và đoạn 1.0.11 (ảnh đại diện + báo cáo), thêm đoạn dưới. Không có IAP mới.

---

```
WHAT'S NEW IN 1.0.12 (in addition to the notes of 1.0.11)

1) Friends: a signed-in (email-linked) player can find another player by
   nickname and send a friend request. Friends can exchange a daily in-game gift,
   visit each other's shop (read-only view) and "raid" it for a small in-game
   reward - no player ever loses currency or items. There is NO free-text chat
   or messaging between players; only nicknames (already shown on public
   profiles/leaderboards) and the fixed in-game actions above are used.

2) World Boss: a server-wide boss (weekly on Sunday plus a daily one). Players
   tap to deal damage; virtual gem rewards unlock at 5 community damage tiers.
   In-game currency only, no cash-out.

3) City Branches: 17 cities on a world map where a player opens branches that
   produce in-game coins, with events, daily quests and a branch leaderboard.
   Branch levels slightly boost the main shop's income. In-game currency only.

4) Visual polish: popup transitions, reward animations, gentler boss hit effect.
```
